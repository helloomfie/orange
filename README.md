# Orange

Orange is a community-built product library focused on everyday household items, starting with produce.

Users contribute products by uploading photos, sticker images, PLU codes, store information, and simple real-world observations. Over time, stronger entries can be reviewed and expanded through a research verification layer.

The goal is to create a calmer, more trustworthy alternative to traditional product discovery systems by focusing on shared product memory, sourcing context, and structured public archives instead of social feeds or aggressive scoring systems.

Current areas of focus include:

- produce tracking
- PLU-based product organization
- sticker and label evidence
- community-contributed entries
- research-reviewed verification
- sourcing transparency
- lightweight metadata systems

Orange is currently in active development.
